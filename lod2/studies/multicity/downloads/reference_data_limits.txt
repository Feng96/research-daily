Government building mesh audit and controlled-experiment candidates

Replay (read-only sources; all outputs stay in this directory):
  sbatch mesh_audit/run_audit.sbatch
or run audit_meshes.py then finalize_meshes.py with the GIS PYTHONPATH above.

training_manifest.json is incremental while audit_complete=false. Do not freeze
training splits until audit_complete=true and split_group has been finalized.
The audit does not create a split, sample synthetic points, or train any model.

Boston: local OBJ XY + [731100,2902900] US survey feet, EPSG2249 to EPSG26986.
Z is NAVD88 US survey feet according to official documentation, converted by
1200/3937. No additional 0.34-degree rotation. Ground is catalog Gnd_El_Ft,
not mesh minimum: model bottoms can penetrate terrain.
Source documentation: https://maps.bostonplans.org/3d/3D_Data_Documentation.pdf
Archives: https://maps.bostonplans.org/3d/Bos3d_BldgModels_20251204_OBJ/
Most current nonflat candidates in these two downloaded tiles have catalog LoD
1.5, not LoD2. The geometry, rather than that label, motivates the controlled
roof task. This does not upgrade them to a certified LoD2 dataset. Mostly 2011
photogrammetry; model/edit/export dates are not acquisition dates.

Philadelphia: public FileGDB has WGS84 XY but unknown physical Z units/datum.
GDB ZScale/ZUnits describe storage precision, not independently verified metres.
Official metadata has blank coordinate-system/altitude detail and blank lineage:
https://www.pasda.psu.edu/uci/FullMetadataDisplay.aspx?file=Philadelphia_Building_3DModels.xml
Only dimensionless per-axis bbox geometry is allowed for controlled training.
This changes aspect ratios, slopes and angles. Do not pool physical normal,
slope, height, or metre accuracy metrics with Boston. Dataset publication label
2015 is not a verified per-building observation epoch. The downloaded data cover
only central Philadelphia, not the whole city.

No authoritative semantic RoofSurface labels were found. roof_triangles are a
mesh-derived upper-envelope proxy: nonvertical triangles (slope <=85 degrees),
four barycentric interior probes all within tolerance of the highest mesh
surface. Flat roofs are preserved; occluded/partly occluded triangles are not
promoted to roof labels. Adjacent coplanar triangles are grouped before treating
their boundaries as structure: raw triangulation edges are not roof ridges.
This proxy may still include exposed sloped architectural surfaces and must not
be described as human roof-face GT. The selection is geometry-driven and does
not inspect any learning/test outcomes.

NPZ interface:
  Boston triangles_m and roof_triangles: [N,3,3], absolute EPSG26986 XY metres,
      NAVD88 Z metres; not local/centered. full_triangles same frame.
  Philadelphia triangles_dimensionless and roof_triangles: [N,3,3], each source
      axis normalized independently to its full-model bounding box.
      full_triangles_projectedXY_rawZ preserves projected XY and unknown raw Z.
  roof_source_face: [N], original polygon/triangle face index.
  roof_plane_id: [N], connected coplanar upper-envelope patch id.
  full_source_face: source face index for each full triangulated surface.
  full_component_id/roof_component_id: vertex-welded source shell component.
      Stable string component identities and parent binding are in manifest.
  upper_envelope_mask/ambiguous_upper_mask: masks over full_triangles.
JSON files preserve original polygon rings (including holes) and metadata.
Manifest footprint_candidate is the roof upper-envelope projection in WORLD
projected XY even for Philadelphia, not its normalized training coordinates.

Stable identities and leakage protection:
  source_id/parent_building_id bind every face, disconnected shell and future
  noisy/augmented/sample realization of a model to the same group. Source archive
  and original-geometry SHA256 are recorded; world geometry fingerprint is
  coordinate-aware, so identical architectural shapes elsewhere do not merge.
  Final split_group unions parent records whose world roof footprints are within
  0.25 metres, conservatively keeping connected row houses together. Freeze
  spatial groups before augmentation, and do not relax adjacency after seeing
  validation losses. Boston pitched candidates come from a single tile; random
  triangle or point splits are invalid geographic generalization tests.

Topology checks report edge incidence after vertex welding and optional existing
vertex collinear noding. Closed edge incidence is necessary but does not verify
self-intersections, winding consistency, nonintersecting shells, or certified
solid validity. Unclosed source meshes are explicitly flagged, not repaired.

All meshes are historical government reference geometry of unverified accuracy,
not certified independent measurement GT. All future mesh-sampled points/noise
must be called simulated controls, never real 3DEP or NAIP observations.
