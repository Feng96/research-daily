适用范围与限制

  • Automatic3DBAG Roofer-lineage teachers, not independent human GT
  • Synthetic point observations, no real AHN/3DEP training claim
  • Exact teacher roof-projection footprint and teacher ground are common priors; registration and footprint errors are not tested
  • No optical CNN or spatial image fusion tested in this ladder
  • No US precision, annotation saving, new architecture or SOTA claim
  • Only screened modules were repeated; screening50 is part of source200, so module selection is exploratory.
  • Three-seed family means average separately reconstructed errors, not the probability-mean ensemble.
  • Source/final use identical12 simulated corruption mechanisms; new cities do not establish real-point-cloud accuracy.
  • Native LoD2 status and edge-incidence diagnostics do not certify watertightness or complete solid validity.
  • Terrain input changed before this round; old-round absolute scores cannot be subtracted to attribute model improvement.
  • Bootstrap intervals omit teacher bias, new-city uncertainty, multiple-comparison selection effects and population seed uncertainty.
  • 真实20栋来自source训练城市的固定ID，teacher与AHN同源；三partition均值是新的真实推理适配，分数未作真实域校准。
  • RGB2023只用于质量预览，原prepared optical_features8仍缺失，主实验没有RGB输入。
